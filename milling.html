Milling – Effective Solutions

Holmlund Contracting handles all types of milling, including steel, concrete, asphalt, and stone, using modern methods and advanced machinery. Our expertise includes disc milling for precise demolition of reinforced concrete, trenches, and hard frost. Hydraulic disc mills ensure faster, safer, and more efficient results compared to traditional methods, enabling selective demolition and quicker site clearance.
